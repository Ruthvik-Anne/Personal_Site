Language-free thought

Research

Paper in progress

Do populations of tiny language-free learners learn what a single learner cannot? A simulated world of reward-modulated Hebbian entities (about 10k weights each, no backprop) tests the claim, with experiments run on Kaggle and a paper in progress.
